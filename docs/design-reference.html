Why Mubeen

Built for the masjid, not around it.

Every feature starts from what an imam, an AV volunteer, or a worshipper actually needs on a Friday — not a generic feature checklist.

🕋

Accurate prayer times

Adhan times calculated from your masjid's exact location and method — iqamah and Jumu'ah times set by your own admins, since those vary by community.

💬

Live khutbah captions

Real-time Arabic-to-English translation streamed to your TV and to any worshipper's phone, with Qur'an recitation matched to the canonical Yusuf Ali translation.

📍

One tap to find a masjid

Search by neighborhood, filter by amenities like parking, wudu facilities, or women's sections, and get directions instantly.

📱

Phone-as-remote

An AV volunteer starts and stops captioning from their own phone. The TV is just a display — no new hardware to install or maintain.

🔒

Scripture-safe by design

Qur'an is never machine-translated live. Recitation is matched against a verified corpus and the official translation is displayed instead.

🕐

Free for your community

Directory listing and prayer times are always free for masjids. No subscriptions required to be found by your community.

How it works

From khateeb to congregation in four steps.

01

Register

Your masjid submits essential details — name, address, times, photos. Verified in under 72 hours.

02

Connect audio

Tap your PA system or use a phone mic near the minbar. No new equipment required.

03

Start captioning

A volunteer taps Start on their phone. Captions appear on the TV and stream to followers instantly.

04

Community follows

Worshippers scan a QR code to follow along on their own phone — no app, no login.
